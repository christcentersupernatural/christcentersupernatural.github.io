David Musau

His story

The Tribute

The next man we are celebrating today is an accomplished Human Resource professional whose passion for developing people is matched only by his love for music. A gifted keyboardist and an emerging guitarist, he has a unique ability to inspire both in the workplace and on the stage. He is a natural leader who believes in bringing out the best in others, and his calm, dependable personality makes him someone people enjoy working and serving alongside. And just in case you're wondering, yes, he currently holds the prestigious, highly contested office of our Presiding Senior Bachelor — a position he occupies with remarkable commitment, though we hear applications for a successor are always welcome!

Beyond his professional and musical talents, he is a man of character, service, and purpose. He continues to lead by example, investing in people, supporting those around him, and using his gifts to make a positive impact in the body of Christ. His dedication, humility, and sense of responsibility have earned him the respect and admiration of many.

Ladies and gentlemen, let us give a warm celebration to David Musau!
