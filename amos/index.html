His story

The Tribute

The man we are celebrating next is a distinguished Mechanical Engineer whose life is marked by excellence, generosity, and an unwavering vision for greatness. He believes that prosperity is a Kingdom principle and is passionate about creating and stewarding wealth that extends far beyond Kenya to impact nations around the world. His generosity reflects his heart for people, and he is always willing to support, encourage, and uplift those around him. He is a man who dreams big, thinks globally, and inspires others to believe that with God, no vision is too great. And then there's his love for politics! Whenever there's a discussion about leadership or national affairs, he somehow ends up sounding like he's already rehearsing a manifesto. Don't be surprised if one day you see his campaign posters.

Above all, he is deeply committed to advancing the Kingdom of God by whatever means God provides. Whether through his profession, his resources, or his influence, he believes every opportunity is a platform to glorify God and serve humanity. His passion for excellence, prosperity, generosity, and the Gospel continues to inspire those around him to dream bigger, serve faithfully, and leave a lasting impact.
