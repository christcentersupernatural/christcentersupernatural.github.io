His story

The Tribute

The man we are celebrating now is a distinguished Project Management professional and the visionary founder of M-Shauri, an innovative fintech platform designed to make financial literacy, investment opportunities, and financial protection as simple and accessible as sending airtime. He is passionate about empowering people to make sound financial decisions and creating solutions that transform lives. Beyond the boardroom, he is a devoted worshipper and an emerging drummer, using his gifts to serve God with excellence. Known for his humility and unwavering loyalty, he is someone people can always count on. And when it comes to commitment, Caleb doesn't just do things halfway — if he tells you he'll handle something, you can almost set your watch by it. In fact, if commitment were a profession, he'd probably be overqualified!

Above all, Caleb is a man of integrity whose life reflects consistency, servant leadership, and faithfulness. Whether leading projects, building innovative financial solutions, serving in worship, or supporting those around him, he gives his very best with excellence and dedication. His quiet strength, dependable character, and passion for seeing others thrive have earned him the respect and admiration of many.
