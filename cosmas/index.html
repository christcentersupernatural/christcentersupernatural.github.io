Profile Six

Cosmas Kariuki

Businessman & Salesman

His story

The Tribute

The man we are celebrating next is a businessman at heart, making his mark in the heart of Nairobi through a thriving shoe business built on hard work, integrity, and exceptional salesmanship. Self-driven and remarkably calm, he approaches every opportunity with confidence and determination. He is also passionate about helping others build wealth, often encouraging people to invest in property, especially land, because, according to him, “they're not making any more of it!” His practical wisdom and entrepreneurial mindset have inspired many to think beyond today and invest in tomorrow. And if you've spent even five minutes with him, you've probably heard him confess the Word of God. In fact, we sometimes wonder whether the Bible has a secret audio version in his voice, because he always has a Scripture ready for every situation!

Beyond business, his greatest passion is advancing the Kingdom of God through soul winning and encouraging others to live by faith. He believes that God's Word is not only meant to be read but also boldly spoken and lived out. His life reflects consistency, humility, and a genuine desire to see others prosper both spiritually and materially. Whether he's sharing business advice, speaking words of faith, or leading someone to Christ, he does it with sincerity and purpose.

  • 📖A Scripture For Every Situation

Ladies and gentlemen, let us warmly celebrate Cosmas Kariuki!
