Gregory Odhiambo

His story

The Tribute

The man we are celebrating next is an accomplished Electrical Engineer with a passion for entrepreneurship and building businesses that stand the test of time. As the founder of Magrega Electronic, he has built a trusted business supplying mobile phones and a wide range of everyday electronic products. His entrepreneurial mindset is driven by a vision for lasting investments, sustainable growth, and creating value that extends beyond today. He believes success is not measured by quick wins but by building something that will outlive him. And when it comes to football, let's just say he doesn't simply watch the game — he coaches from the living room! The players may not hear him, but that has never stopped him from giving world-class tactical instructions to the television.

Beyond his success in business, he is a man of integrity, discipline, and purpose. His commitment to excellence, coupled with his passion for creating opportunities through business and investment, continues to inspire those around him. He believes that with vision, consistency, and hard work, ordinary ideas can become extraordinary enterprises.
