Pastor Phronesis Murimi

Actuarial Scientist

His story

The Tribute

The man we are celebrating next is a brilliant Actuarial Scientist with a sharp analytical mind and an entrepreneurial spirit. He believes that businesses are not just built to generate income but to create value and transform lives. As the founder of GG Cleaning Services and the driving force behind Victors Detergents and Chemicals, he has demonstrated that faith and enterprise can thrive together. Despite his busy schedule as a businessman, he remains approachable, visionary, and always ready to encourage others to pursue excellence. But if you really want to see him under pressure, don't give him a business challenge — just let Manchester United play! During those 90 minutes, his prayer life reaches another level, and every misplaced pass becomes a serious prayer request!

Above all, he is a devoted servant of God, serving as a full-time pastor and lead intercessor with an unwavering belief that every matter should first be taken to God in prayer. He has dedicated his life to shepherding God's people, standing in the gap through intercession, and inspiring many to live lives of faith, purpose, and obedience. His life beautifully reflects the balance of spiritual devotion, entrepreneurial excellence, and servant leadership.

  • Founder, GG Cleaning Services
  • Victors Detergents & Chemicals
  • Lead Intercessor

Ladies and gentlemen, let us rise and celebrate Pastor Phronesis Murimi!
